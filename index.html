About Us

LEAPS Labor Services offers a full range of mechanical, electrical, structural and civil works. Our Company’s story started in 2013 when we were able to see the need for competent workforce to fulfill the expanding industrial needs of the Visayas area.

Born from the desire to solve the problems and be a catalyst for growth our core management team gathered the most skilled and experienced workforce to take on whatever industrial challenges that are present on the market.

Why Us?

Mission

At LEAPS Labor Services Company, Our mission is to provide reliable, high-quality labor and services to our clients, We are committed to delivering exceptional customer services while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and safety. We believe in fostering a culture of teamwork, respect, and innovation to positively impact our clients, employees, and communities.

Vision

Our vision is to be the industry’s leading provider of labor and services, recognized for our commitment to excellence, reliability, and innovation. We strive to build long-term relationships with our clients, employees, and partners, and to be a trusted partner in their success. We are dedicated to creating a workplace that values diversity, promotes personal and professional growth, and inspires our team to achieve their full potential.

Values

At LEAPS Labor Services, we are committed to delivering the best solutions for our clients, from project conception to completion, regardless of size. Through careful planning, precise execution, continuous workforce training, and unwavering commitment to health and safety, we ensure every project meets the highest local and international standards while maintaining a safe and productive work environment for our people.
